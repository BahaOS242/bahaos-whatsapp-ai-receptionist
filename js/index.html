The patient journey

One conversation, five jobs

Each message the AI sends is doing one of these five jobs, in roughly this order, for every real conversation.

01

Answer

Answers common questions — hours, pricing, insurance — from the business's own knowledge base.

02

Qualify

Picks up on intent and captures contact details as a lead, even mid-conversation.

03

Book

Collects service, date, time, name, and phone — never re-asking for what it already knows.

04

Follow up

Handles reschedules and cancellations without losing the thread of the conversation.

05

Escalate

Recognizes when it should stop guessing and hand off to a person.
