In order, every conversation

Five jobs. Zero missed beats.

Each message the AI sends is doing one of these jobs, in roughly this order. It keeps time like a group moving down Bay Street, so your staff don't have to.

1

Answer

Hours, pricing and insurance, straight from the business's own knowledge base.

"Do you take insurance?" → plan info, in seconds

2

Qualify

Picks up on intent and captures contact details as a lead, even mid-conversation.

"Haven't been in 2 yrs" → new patient

3

Book

Service, date, time, name and phone. It never re-asks for what it already knows.

"Cleaning Friday 2pm" → asks only for name

4

Follow up

Handles reschedules and cancellations without losing the thread of the conversation.

"Cancel Thursday" → slot reopened

5

Escalate

Recognizes when it should stop guessing and hand off to a person.

"Speak to someone" → front desk, flagged

How is this calculated?

Recover

Missed bookings/month × Recovery rate × Avg. revenue per new customer × 12

Retain

Active customers × (Expected retention − Current retention) × Avg. annual revenue per retained customer

Reactivate

Inactive customers × Reactivation rate × Revenue per reactivated customer

Net impact & ROI

Total impact − ($300 × 12) = Net impact. Net impact ÷ $3,600 = ROI multiple.

These are illustrative planning assumptions, not actual customer results. Actual outcomes depend on message volume, booking conversion, customer value, retention behavior, follow-up effectiveness, and the business's existing processes. Recover, Retain, and Reactivate are modeled as separate customer populations (new bookings, existing active customers, and lapsed customers). If your real numbers overlap between categories, adjust the inputs so the same customer isn't counted twice.
